# Access Management Policy

This is a fictitious policy created only for the portfolio demo.

## New access
Access to corporate systems must be requested through the Service Desk. The request must identify the target system, requested role or permission level, business justification, manager approval, and expected duration when access is temporary.

## Least privilege
Permissions should follow the principle of least privilege. Administrators must grant only the access required for the approved business activity.

## Review and removal
Privileged access must be reviewed periodically. Access must be removed or adjusted when an employee changes role, leaves the company, or no longer has a valid business need.

## Incidents
Suspected unauthorized access must be reported immediately to IT Security. Credentials must never be shared between users.
