# Purchasing Policy

This is a fictitious policy created only for the portfolio demo.

## Purchase requests
A purchase request must contain the item or service description, quantity, estimated value, business justification, cost center, and requester information.

## Approval
Requests above the department's delegated limit require additional approval from Finance. IT-related purchases must also be technically validated by IT before an order is placed.

## Supplier selection
Purchases should use approved suppliers when possible. New suppliers require registration and basic compliance validation before receiving an order.

## Records
Purchase approvals, quotations, orders, and delivery evidence must be retained according to the organization's document-retention rules.
