# IT Equipment Request Policy

This is a fictitious policy created only for the portfolio demo.

## Requesting a notebook
Employees who need a new notebook must open an equipment request with the IT Service Desk. The request must include the employee name, department, business justification, required software, and desired delivery date.

The employee's manager must approve the request before IT evaluates inventory and technical requirements. Standard equipment should be delivered within five business days after approval when inventory is available. Non-standard configurations may require purchasing approval and can take longer.

## Replacement
A notebook may be replaced when it is no longer supported, has a confirmed hardware failure, or no longer meets an approved business requirement. IT must validate the condition before replacement.

## Security
All company notebooks must use approved endpoint protection, disk encryption, operating-system updates, and corporate access controls. Users must not disable security controls without written authorization from IT Security.
