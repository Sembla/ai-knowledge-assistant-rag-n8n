# IT Equipment Request Policy

This fictional policy is used only for the portfolio demonstration.

Employees who need a new notebook, monitor, headset or other IT equipment must open a request with the IT service desk. The request must include the employee name, department, equipment type, business justification and desired delivery date.

The employee's manager must approve the request before purchasing. After approval, IT validates the technical specification and Procurement checks approved suppliers and budget availability.

Standard requests should be submitted at least 10 business days before the equipment is needed. Urgent requests must include the operational impact and require manager approval.
